01 / SUMMARY

Executive summary

Two parallel workstreams. Foundation recovery on the pages you have. Commercial growth on the pages you need.

Your traffic decline over the past twelve months is real and substantial — but the majority of what was lost was informational reference traffic from material specification pages (Q345, ADC12, ST37, etc.), much of it from engineers overseas. That traffic is worth recovering on its own merits, but it was never the lead engine.

The commercial layer of your site — the service hubs, country pages, and industry verticals that drive actual buyer inquiry — has been thin and under-ranked for years. The 60-day sprint rebuilds that layer while also addressing the recoverable portion of the lost traffic.

The contracted scope is 45 pages. We're delivering 85, structured into six blocks. Every additional page is included at no extra cost and earns its place by either ranking inside the engagement window or establishing positioning that converts in the months that follow.

Material content refresh

10 pages · No additional cost

Above the contracted scope at no additional cost. These ten pages drove 66,407 clicks over the past 16 months — roughly 64% of all historical traffic to the site. They still rank on page one for some material specification queries, but click-through has eroded significantly. They have URL history, established backlinks, and prior indexing — which makes them the only pages on the site with a realistic recovery shot inside the 60-day engagement window.

08 / OUTCOMES

What success looks like

Realistic expectations across the engagement window and the months that follow. SEO timing is non-linear: most of the work landed in the 60 days produces compounding signal that becomes visible only after Google has crawled, indexed, and evaluated the new site.

By Day 30

Foundation live

  • Existing 25 pages with on-page optimization deployed to the current site
  • Briefs and drafts complete for all 85 pages
  • Technical SEO audit and algorithm/penalty diagnosis delivered
  • Tracking infrastructure live and reporting
  • Some early position movement possible on existing pages, but most signal is post-launch
By Day 60

New site indexing

  • New site launched with all 85 pages live
  • Redirect map deployed and validated
  • Google indexing in progress across all new pages
  • Early ranking signal on low-difficulty pages (welding, plasma cutting, aerospace sheet metal, military CNC)
  • End-of-sprint diagnostic with phase 2 recommendation delivered
Day 60 — 180

Compounding signal

  • Higher-difficulty pillars (laser cutting, powder coating) begin ranking
  • Country grid moves to page-one positions for low-competition queries
  • Material content refresh recovers measurable click volume on high-impression pages
  • US service area shows ranking signal worth phase 2 investment
  • Industry verticals begin driving qualified inquiry
A note on the recovery curve The traffic decline took roughly twelve months to play out. Recovery is on a similar arc — not because the work takes that long, but because Google needs time to re-evaluate the site after structural changes. We expect positive momentum visible in the back half of the engagement and accelerating through months three and four. Anyone promising "all traffic back in 60 days" on a site rebuild is overpromising. What we're delivering inside the window is the foundation that compounds.
09 / CONTEXT

Considerations and dependencies

Items worth flagging up front so there are no surprises during the engagement.

Dependency · Critical

Site build timing

Most of the new content goes live with Kaz's site launch. If the build slips, the post-launch indexing window inside the engagement compresses. We're working in lock-step with Kaz to maintain the schedule, and the existing-page optimization in Block 02 is structured to ship to the current site immediately so progress is not gated by launch.

Decision Required

Medical and defense positioning

Pages targeting ISO 13485 or ITAR keywords will attract inbound qualified on those certifications. Until those certs are in process or confirmed, capability-only or partner-network framing protects the brand. Detail in the Decisions section below.

Industry Trend

AI Overviews on material queries

Some of the material specification SERPs that previously drove your highest-volume traffic are now answered by Google's AI Overview directly above the organic results. A portion of that lost click volume may not be fully recoverable regardless of content quality. The material refresh in Block 05 prioritizes pages where AI Overview has lowest footprint and traditional click capture remains strongest.

Internal Architecture

Hub and spoke discipline

The site will run three parallel hub-and-spoke structures after launch: existing service hubs and country spokes, new finishing and sheet metal pillars with sub-service spokes, and the US service area hub with city spokes. Internal linking is locked into a predetermined architecture to prevent any of these from cannibalizing the others.
